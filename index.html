Who We Are

Building a transformed, self-reliant youth community

Registered with the Nakuru district office, Bright Wellness Youth CBO exists to promote oneness and encourage one another — mutually, socially, physically and financially.

🌱 Our Vision

A transformed, self-reliant, and healthy youth community actively shaping positive change.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower and support youth through skills development, health awareness, and community engagement for a better future.

"EMPOWER THE YOUTH, TRANSFORM THE WORLD"

What members enjoy

Membership at Bright Wellness

Becoming a member is simple. Here's what you need to know.

✅Eligibility

  • Must be 18 years and above (of legal age)
  • Must be a resident of Nakuru

🤝Admission

  • Accepted by 60% of members
  • Pay the one-time registration fee
Registration Fee: Ksh. 1,000

⭐Member Rights

  • Attend meetings & take part in decision making
  • Fair discipline process with a right of appeal
  • Full participation in the life of the organization
